Welcome to the OpenPlan hospital demand model outputs app.

This tool is designed to help facilitate a demand modelling process, primarily to support the development of robust, local proposals for the New Hospital Programme (NHP).

The NHP requires estimates of future activity levels to inform the design of a new hospital.
This tool is designed to help determine how hospital activity might change in the years to come (relative to a baseline year) and to provide a high-level view of the physical capacity required to meet that demand.

This app allows results from modelled scenarios to be viewed and downloaded.

For more information or help, please visit [the OpenPlan hospital demand model documentation](https://connect.strategyunitwm.nhs.uk/nhp/project_information/), or contact the [MLCSU NHP Analytics mailbox](mailto:mlcsu.nhpanalytics@nhs.net).
